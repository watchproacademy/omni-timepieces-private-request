# Omni — U.S. smaller-market targeting brief

Research checked October 1, 2026. Planning recommendations only; no advertising has been launched.

## Confirmed direction

- Buyers in the United States only.
- Rolex, Audemars Piguet, and Patek Philippe.
- Prioritize smaller markets with limited convenient access to the desired watches.
- Exclude Miami, Los Angeles, and New York, including their surrounding metro areas when configuring the account.
- Latest initial test budget range: $15–$50 total, with $15 proposed first. Earlier monthly budget discussion is superseded for the launch.
- Prepare only. Do not activate until the owner separately confirms the final campaign.

## Recommendation

Start forecasting Northwest Arkansas first, Boise/Eagle second, and Huntsville/Madison third. For the proposed $15 test, use Bentonville/Rogers only. Other clusters are future research candidates, not approved launch targets. These are provisional priorities, not a data-proven ranking of lead quality or ad competition.

The research supports a test of access to specific watches in smaller markets. It does not establish that buyers have no access to watches. Every shortlisted cluster has existing Rolex retail and secondary-market options. National online dealers can also compete for these buyers. Limited physical retail is therefore a targeting hypothesis, not proof of inexpensive clicks or easy sales.

## Campaign geography

Use one Search campaign with three brand ad groups sharing a budget. Add the selected U.S. cities as positive targets; confirm the exact recognized boundaries in Google Ads before launch. Add adjacent cities only when they are part of the selected cluster. Avoid a broad radius that unintentionally covers unrelated areas.

Use “Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations.” Do not use location interest targeting. Do not add a nationwide U.S. positive target alongside the cities. Explicitly exclude the Miami, Los Angeles, and New York metro areas, resolving the appropriate boundaries in the account. Google location detection is signal-based, so verify actual geographic delivery after launch. [Google location guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9376662?hl=en)

Keep other large luxury hubs outside the initial positive target list. This does not require excluding entire states such as Florida, California, or New York; smaller cities there can be researched separately later.

## Brand-specific buyer intent

- Rolex: specific-model purchase searches such as Submariner, GMT-Master II, and Daytona. Reference-level terms only where sourcing and margin are workable.
- Audemars Piguet: Royal Oak purchase searches, with the full brand name to reduce ambiguity. Offshore can be added if commercially appropriate.
- Patek Philippe: Nautilus and Aquanaut purchase searches; other collections can be introduced based on sourcing and demand.

All three brands remain in scope. Do not create nine separate city-by-brand campaigns on this budget. A shared campaign simplifies the initial test, although spending may skew toward one brand and some groups may get no traffic. A $15 sample cannot reliably compare brands. Watch the distribution rather than assuming an equal split.

## Positioning to test

“Your next timepiece. Sourced personally.”

“Searching for a specific Rolex, Audemars Piguet, or Patek Philippe? Share your reference, budget, and timing. Omni's private desk will review your request and help source suitable options.”

The offer should make remote buying feel personal and clear. Show actual watch details, condition, pricing, verification, and delivery arrangements when presenting an option. Do not claim that the customer's city has no dealers, that Omni has an authorized-brand relationship, or that a watch is in stock without evidence.

## Validation before activation

1. Obtain city-level keyword forecasts and expected click-cost ranges in the ad account. If a cluster is too small, combine only relevant smaller-market locations or sequence the test. Do not manufacture demand estimates.
2. Confirm sourceable references and margins for all three brands. Do not assume household income establishes demand for AP/Patek.
3. Complete conversion and lead-delivery checks described in the main launch kit.
4. Verify that a true $15 campaign total cap can be configured, and present final settings for separate activation confirmation. Do not launch automatically.

Report leads and qualified buyer conversations by city cluster and brand. Keep the user's smaller-market strategy unless they authorize a change. Lead quality and sales outcomes determine expansion; retailer counts alone do not.
