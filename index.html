OMNI TIMEPIECES

Privacy

We collect the watch preferences, budget, trade-in information, location, and contact details you provide so we can respond to and fulfill your private request.

Your information is used by our private desk and, when necessary, trusted dealers or service partners involved in locating, evaluating, or delivering your watch. We do not sell your personal information.

You may ask us to correct or delete your request information by contacting your concierge. We retain request information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal obligations.

OMNI TIMEPIECES

Terms

A private request is an invitation to locate a watch; it is not a purchase agreement or guarantee of availability. Pricing, condition, provenance, and delivery terms are confirmed before any acquisition.

Trade-in expectations are not final appraisals. Final values require inspection and verification. The 24-hour statement describes our typical ability to locate most watches, not a guaranteed delivery time.

Payment, authentication, insurance, returns, and shipping terms will be presented for approval before a transaction is completed.
